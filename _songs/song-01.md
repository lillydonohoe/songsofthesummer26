I chose the song Man I Need by Olivia Dean and I gave it a 5 star rating. This song came out August 15th, 2025 but just recently became very popular. This song has a very forward and confident vibe to it. 


The lyrics to this song are about never settling and knowing your worth. Olivia Dean is very good with her lyrics and gets such a confident message across in such an upbeat song. THis song reminds me of being in Madison Square Garden at her concert this summer. She is an amazing performer and this song was the final song. This song will always be my number one, it is truly a 5 star song in my opinion.
